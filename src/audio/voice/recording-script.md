# Ashen Tangent voice recording script

In ElevenLabs Text to Speech, choose a voice and paste the quoted lines one clip at a time. Keep the acting directions in this sheet as a reference. Export each take as its named MP3 or WAV beside this script. Leave the audio dry so the game can mix it with the soundtrack.

For a quick first pass, record the four files ending in `_root`. The game will display the written lines for any clips still in progress.

Suggested pronunciation. **Astrolabe** sounds like AS-truh-layb, **Aurelius** like aw-REE-lee-us, **Vespera** like VES-per-uh, **Malakor** like MAL-uh-kor, and **Vaelorath** like VAY-loh-rath.

## Archmage Aurelius

Older, warm, low, and slightly weathered. Speak like a patient teacher with a village to protect. Use the same voice as the opening cinematic.

### aurelius_root.mp3

> The Celestial Astrolabe shields Solstice Haven, but its light is fading. Recover Vespera’s Prism, Malakor’s Seal, and Vaelorath’s Crest from the three guardians. Start with Vespera in the Whispering Glade to learn how Sine and Cosine work together. Inspect each relic after the fight, then bring all three to the Astrolabe beside me.

### aurelius_calculus_lesson.mp3

> When Vespera casts sine, sine of theta, use cosine, cosine of theta. Cosine gives the slope of sine, so the matching sigil can break her ward. Guided Math equips it for you. With Guided Math off, press Q or tap Sigils to choose it, then cast at her.

### aurelius_advanced_lesson.mp3

> Tangent of theta races towards vertical asymptotes, countered only by secant squared of theta. And Vaelorath's aerial secant of theta trajectory requires secant of theta times tangent of theta. Master all six trigonometric derivatives, and the entire valley will revere your resonance!

### aurelius_quest_hub.mp3

> Three cosmic anomalies threaten the valley. Which undertaking calls to your blade?

### aurelius_stats_hub.mp3

> Your Astral Insight resonates deeply. Channel your unspent Stat Points to empower your journey across the valley.

### aurelius_restored.mp3

> The Celestial Astrolabe shines again. Solstice Haven is safe, Spellblade.

## Theron the Smith

A sturdy, gravelly voice with a quick grin. He talks like a craftsman speaking to an apprentice across his workbench.

### blacksmith_root.mp3

> Welcome to my forge, traveler. A blade is only as sharp as the math behind it. If your strike fails to match the ward's derivative, the harmonic backlash will jar your bones! Keep your sigil attuned, and strike true.

### blacksmith_lore.mp3

> I temper steel using ore mined from the Skyridge foothills and cooled in the northern brook. Archmage Aurelius imbues the metal with astrolabe sigils so your blade can slice through trigonometric shields. Bring me two pieces of Mithril Ore and one Ancient Bone from the wilds, and I will temper your Spellblade up to Tier Five, adding ten percent damage each time.

## Lyra the Herbalist

A warm, clear voice with an easy pace. Her friendliness sounds lived-in, and she gets matter-of-fact when giving directions.

### herbalist_root.mp3

> The meadow flowers are blooming with radiant energy today! If you are injured from your travels, drink from your Crimson Flask. Press R on keyboard, or tap the flask icon. You can rest by the town fountain anytime to recover.

### herbalist_lore.mp3

> Cross the north gate bridge and take the left fork to Whispering Glade. Two Moonbell blossoms grow along the trail before Vespera's grove. The Phase Wolves there carry Phase Essence. Attune to cosine of theta to break their sine of theta wards. Bring me two Moonbell blossoms and one Phase Essence for another Crimson Flask charge.

## Eldrin the Scout

Alert and practical, with a lean, steady delivery. Give each route or warning enough space to register.

### scout_root.mp3

> Greetings, traveler! Follow the road through the south gate to the Sunken Sun-Ruins. For Whispering Glade or Skyridge Peak, cross the bridge at the north gate. The left fork leads to the glade, and the right fork climbs to the peak.

### scout_advice.mp3

> Monsters telegraph their deadly magic attacks! Look for glowing runic circles forming on the grass under your feet. When the outer ring collapses, sprint with Shift, jump with Space, or dodge roll with C before impact!

## Opening cinematic

Save this take separately as `opening_aurelius.mp3`. It goes into the film mix. Read the dialogue as one performance, starting with quiet disbelief and ending with a direct request.

> The Astrolabe has gone dark. Its ward is breaking. Vespera, Malakor, Vaelorath… they’re waking. Spellblade, find me at the gazebo. Help me restore the light that keeps Solstice Haven safe.
